## When editing can help
- Editing removes an obstacle that hides an ability; it does not add one. Go ahead only if a removable failure
  dominates and `acc when finished` is far above `acc`.
- Screened so far (`cases/screening`): loops dominate only in small Qwen3.5 models in thinking mode (0.8B strongly,
  2B moderately); Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B, Qwen3.5-4B, R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B, SmolLM3-3B and Phi-4-mini-reasoning show no
  removable failure on GSM8K/MATH train (the post's Gemma 3 270M follow-up is the same negative case). On GSM8K,
  IFEval and MMLU-Pro: OpenReasoning-Nemotron-1.5B loops (13% of GSM8K answers); Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507,
  LFM2.5-1.2B-Thinking, Granite-3.3-2B, Phi-4-mini, OLMo-2-1B, SmolLM2-1.7B and Qwen2.5-1.5B show only reasoning
  errors and unboxed answers.
- Sycophancy (`bench:mmlupro500_sug`: the question plus a user's wrong guess) is the second obstacle found: the
  share of answers taking the guess rises from ~5% to 61-68% in models that answer at once (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct,
  Qwen3-0.6B no-think; Qwen3.5-0.8B no-think 39%), but only to 9-21% in models that reason first (Qwen3-1.7B,
  Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507), where an edit has little to remove.

## Method
- The loop edit: for each chosen layer, `W_down -= alpha * u (u^T W_down)[:, top-k]`, with `u` the residual
  direction separating loop continuations from normal reasoning (mean difference over matched pairs from the model's
  own correct traces) and the top-k MLP neurons writing most along `u`. Layers 15 and 19 of 24, k = 64, alpha = 6
  for both Qwen3.5 sizes; the 0.8B case reproduces the post's result independently.
- Contrast pairs (same prefix, normal vs failure continuation) give a direction stable across layers (split-half
  cosine > 0.9); the writer analysis localizes the edit, and within a model family the whole recipe transfers.
- **Selectivity on held-out pairs is a cheap pre-screen**: good edits lower the failure continuation's log-prob
  (~0.1 nats/token) and leave the normal one nearly unchanged (< 0.02); a drop of the normal log-prob predicts a
  loss in generation.
- Never edit the last layer's MLP even when it is the largest writer: it is entangled with next-token prediction.
- Dose is the main knob; larger models saturate at lower alpha. Adding layers does not help once the failure share
  is small.
- Always run the norm-matched random orthogonal direction: it separates a direction-specific effect from a generic
  perturbation (null in every case so far).
- Removing loops is not math-specific: the edits, selected on math practice only, lift IFEval, MMLU-Pro and BBH,
  where loops cost even more (2B: 41% of MMLU-Pro and 48% of BBH answers loop to the token limit; 0.8B: 91% cut on
  MMLU-Pro). It does not force early stopping: correct answers get longer, because harder problems now finish.
- Always measure the benchmark's failure kinds first: the loop share of a model differs a lot between benchmarks, so
  screening on math alone can miss a loop obstacle elsewhere.
- **The key edit, for behaviors that copy content from the prompt** (sycophancy: the answer copies the user's letter).
  Such a behavior is not one residual direction: behavior pairs are unstable, and a prompt-contrast direction
  (stable 0.99) ablated from MLP writers or o_proj is null. Zeroing the copy heads (found by direct logit
  attribution) works but costs the plain answer, since they copy any chosen option. What works: remove from the
  heads' key input (`dir_ablate` side "in" on k_proj, alpha 1, the second half of the layers) the direction telling
  the source position from its competitors (the note's letter minus the option letters; the copied content cancels).
  The heads keep reading the options but can no longer pick out the user's letter. Qwen2.5-1.5B: +11.4 +- 1.7 with
  a wrong guess, +11.6 on five unseen phrasings, plain MMLU-Pro / GSM8K / IFEval unchanged; on ARC-Challenge (another
  source, never used for fitting) 30.4% -> 67.4% with a wrong guess (+37.0 +- 2.4), i.e. back to the plain 68.2%.
- A teacher-forced answer start ("The correct answer is: \boxed{") is a fast screen (minutes) but overstates the
  effect for models that reason before answering: check the failure's share in real generation first.
- Benchmark variants that share item ids (plain / with a guess) must be paired by set (`case._base_run_for`).

## Sycophancy edit: what it costs and where it stops
- It makes the answer independent of the user's opinion, both ways: with a right guess accuracy falls to near the
  plain level (Qwen2.5 75.8% -> 32.8%, plain 21.8%; Qwen3-0.6B 81.4% -> 65.8%). Report both directions.
- Weaker in Qwen3-0.6B (+5.0 +- 1.5; guess share 68% -> 50%; plain MMLU-Pro -3.0 +- 1.9), null in Qwen3-1.7B
  no-think (+2.7 +- 2.7 DEV) and Qwen3-4B-Thinking (-0.8 +- 2.4 DEV): models that reason first are pulled inside
  their reasoning, not by a copy at the answer.
- On GSM8K with a wrong number the models are hardly sycophantic (2-6% take it); the edit costs -2.6 / -3.6 (n.s.).

## Tried and not confirmed (do not repeat without a new idea)
Paired gains on DEV128 / HOLDOUT256 after the loop edit (2B unless noted).
- **Conclude once the answer is found** (contrast `conclude`): DEV +1.6, HOLDOUT +0.8 ± 2.0. The direction is stable
  but the edit is not selective: it lowers the closing continuation too. After loop removal the 2B's remaining cut
  answers (18% at 32k tokens) are long non-repetitive reasoning, not a single removable behavior.
- **Correct vs wrong reasoning** (contrast `correct`, Qwen3-0.6B, pass@8 96% vs pass@1 76%): split-half stability of
  the direction is negative (-0.15 to -0.39) and the separation < 0.55; "being right" is not one direction.
- **Second-guessing** (contrast `secondguess`: a wrong answer that had stated the right one, Qwen3-0.6B, 5% of
  samples): the direction is very stable (0.95) and separated, yet the edit is null at alpha 3 (-0.7 ± 1.4, random
  +1.1 ± 1.2) and harmful at alpha 6 (-5.8 ± 1.8, n = 8 per problem). The share is only 2-2.4% in Qwen3-1.7B,
  R1-Distill-1.5B and SmolLM3-3B. Even the prompt "trust a careful result" does nothing (-0.5 ± 1.3).
- **Prompt hints as the ceiling for baking a prompt into weights**: "keep reasoning concise" on the loop-fixed 2B
  gives +1.8 ± 1.0 (cut 18.6% -> 16.9%); too small to be worth writing into the weights.
- **Whole-model transforms on loop-free Qwen3** (0.6B, DEV128 x 8 samples): extrapolating post-training,
  `W + a (W - W_pretrained)`, a = 0.2 +0.1 ± 1.6 (1.7B -1.1 ± 0.9), a = -0.1 +0.3 ± 1.3, a = 0.5 runs away into long
  outputs; attention temperature (all q_norm x 1.1 / 1.25) +0.4 / +0.5; sampling temperature 0.4 -0.6; a logit
  penalty on "Wait"/"Alternatively" (thought switching) -1.4 ± 1.2 (R1-1.5B -0.6 ± 0.9). Their errors are reasoning
  errors; nothing global moves them.
- **Attention temperature of one layer for long context** (Qwen3-0.6B, RULER-style 30k needles): q_norm of layer 6
  x 1.5 lifts single-key retrieval on fresh problems 84.0% -> 91.5% (+7.5 ± 1.7) without hurting math or MMLU-Pro,
  but every per-layer scale that helps single-key retrieval lowers multi-value retrieval (L6 -4.0 ± 1.3 recall);
  across 48 single-layer configs none lifts multi-value. The model sits at a joint optimum: a trade-off, not a gain.
  Search two objectives jointly from the start.
- **Language of reasoning** (MGSM, 11 languages): Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B and Qwen3.5-4B already think in English or reason
  equally well in the question's language; "think in English" changes nothing or hurts (0.6B -4.9 ± 1.8). The
  low-resource gap (Swahili 4-10%) is not understanding the question, which an edit cannot supply.
- **Answer format** (instruct models SmolLM2/OLMo2/Qwen2.5 do not box answers; half of SmolLM2's unboxed GSM8K
  answers are right): an edit would only raise a strict grader's score, not the model; graders must extract leniently.
- **Too-short reasoning** (ThinkEdit's failure): absent here, short traces are the most accurate ones (0.6B 95%,
  R1-1.5B 100% vs 54% / 76% for traces over 6k characters).
- **Instruction following** (synthetic IF practice): the model already follows 94% of the checkable constraints,
  and its IFEval failures are scattered across constraint types.
- **Non-thinking mode** (0.8B): wrong answers are genuine reasoning errors, no removable failure. Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B
  already reason before answering; "reason step by step" moves MMLU-Pro / BBH by -3 to +3 (300 items), so there is
  no hidden "answers too fast" gap. Their "no answer" share (0.6B: 23-34%) is unboxed answers, a grader matter.
- **LASER** (rank reduction of one MLP matrix, research op): 15 layer/matrix/rank configurations are flat or
  negative on DEV; the best (L20 down_proj, keep 50% of the rank) HOLDOUT +2.7 ± 2.2, not significant.
- **Decoding instead of weights**: repetition penalty 1.1 on the unedited 2B gives +11.7 ± 3.8 on DEV, the weight
  edit +17.2 ± 3.7; the edit is better and needs no change to the sampler, but most of the loop gain is reachable
  by decoding.

## Engineering
- One job per GPU (tools/queue.sh starts a worker per GPU, so N GPUs run N experiments at once). One 24 GB GPU: vLLM startup ~4 min; a looping 0.8B at 32k tokens ~3,000 tok/s (128 problems ~15-30 min,
  MATH-500 ~1.5 h). Probes take minutes.
- Compare edits only paired; unpaired passes of 128 answers are ~4 points noisy.
